It claims the MonitoringClass "{{ .Values.class.name }}" (pmm-client {{ .Values.class.agentVersion }}):

  kubectl get monitoringclass {{ .Values.class.name }}

To send an Instance's metrics and Query Analytics to a PMM 3 server, in the
Instance's namespace:

  1. Store a PMM 3 service-account token under the key token:
       kubectl -n <namespace> create secret generic pmm-prod-token --from-literal=token=<token>
  2. Create a MonitoringDestination of class "{{ .Values.class.name }}" with parameters.url and
     authentication mode ServiceAccountToken, credentialsSecretRef pmm-prod-token,
     and wait until it is Ready:
       kubectl -n <namespace> get monitoringdestination
  3. List the destination under the Instance's spec.monitoring.destinations[].

See https://github.com/openeverest/monitoring-pmm#readme for details.
